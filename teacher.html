Overview

Credit consumption across every service in your organization, and whether your credits will last until the current term ends on July 31, 2027.

Most school organizations had their credits reset to 50,000 on August 1, 2025. The projection uses your actual balance and the credits used since then.

Credits used are read from ArcGIS Online's usage reporting, the same source as the Credits tab of your organization's status dashboard, and cover every service: storage, analysis, geocoding, notebooks and so on. The balance is the organization's available credits as ArcGIS Online reports it right now. Average per day = credits used since August 1, 2025 ÷ days elapsed. The projection assumes that average continues; heavy one-time work (a large analysis or geocoding job) can make it look higher than your steady rate, so the last-30-days pace is shown for comparison. Credits purchased or added during the term are included in the balance but not in the 50,000 starting amount.

Recycle bin

i
We recommend turning off the recycle bin to save credits.
When the recycle bin is on, deleted items wait there for at least 14 days before they're removed for good, and they keep counting toward your storage the whole time.

Items in the recycle bin

Check what's waiting in the recycle bin, then empty it to delete those items permanently and stop paying to store them.

ItemOwnerTypeSize (MB)Credits per month
Credits per month estimates what each item costs while it sits in the recycle bin: hosted feature layers at the feature storage rate (2.4 credits per 10 MB per month) and everything else at 1.2 credits per GB per month. "Credits saved" after emptying assumes the items would otherwise have stayed in the recycle bin for one month. Emptying the bin deletes items permanently: they can't be restored.

Looking at:

Top 25 feature layers to review

Views (12 months) and Views (30 days) are the usage counts from each item's Usage tab. For a hosted feature layer, ArcGIS counts usage as requests to the layer: one map view can make several requests. They're read from ArcGIS Online's usage reporting; "n/a" means the count couldn't be read, and lifetime views are used for ranking instead. Lifetime views (numViews) is the item's own numViews value, read directly from the item when the scan ran; opening the item page adds to it. Est. credits / yr = feature data × 2.4 credits per 10 MB per month × 12, plus attachments at the file storage rate (1.2 credits per GB per month × 12). Attachments are measured and removed from the layer's reported size. Credits per view = estimated credits per year ÷ (views in the last 12 months + 1). Before deleting anything, check the item page's usage, the maps and apps that use it, and with its owner. Consider exporting a backup first. Delete-protected items are marked.
